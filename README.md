# Sales Forecasting & Revenue Planning Engine

## Business Objective

A practical commercial planning model designed to help sales leaders forecast revenue, evaluate target achievement and identify future sales gaps.

## What This Project Addresses

Sales leaders need to answer three critical questions:

1. What revenue are we likely to achieve?
2. Are we on track against target?
3. What additional sales effort is required to close the gap?

This model is designed to provide a structured approach to answering these questions using historical sales performance, growth assumptions and business targets.

## Key Business Outputs

- Revenue Forecast
- Target vs. Forecast
- Growth %
- Sales Gap
- Required Run Rate
- Channel / Region Performance
- Management Action Indicators

## Commercial Applications

The model can support:

- Annual and monthly sales planning
- GT / MT / Retail forecasting
- Regional target allocation
- Sales performance reviews
- Gap-to-target analysis
- Management decision-making
